One focused job, done plainly.

BP Exchange is a payment-confirmation submission app. It collects what the team needs to review a payment, and nothing is promised beyond that.

  • Payment details form

    Enter the details of your payment through a structured form in the app.

  • Screenshot or photo as proof

    Attach an image that supports your payment details.

  • Manual team review

    Each submission is reviewed by people on the BP Exchange team, not by an automated check.

  • A straightforward process

    Three steps from opening the app to sending your submission.

How it works

  1. Enter payment details

    Fill in the payment information requested in the app.

  2. Attach proof

    Add a screenshot or photo that supports those details.

  3. Submit for manual review

    Send the confirmation to the BP Exchange team, who review it and follow up according to their process.

Please note: submitting a confirmation does not mean your payment has been verified or accepted. That decision rests with the BP Exchange team after review.

Frequently asked questions

What is BP Exchange?

BP Exchange is an Android app for submitting payment details and supporting proof so the BP Exchange team can review them manually.

How do I submit payment proof?

Open the app, enter your payment details, attach a screenshot or photo, and submit the confirmation.

Does submitting proof mean my payment is verified?

No. Submitting proof only sends it for review. A payment is not verified or accepted until the team has reviewed it and follows up.

Who reviews my submission?

People on the BP Exchange team review submissions manually. The team follows its own verification process.

How can I request account or data deletion?

Use the “Request account deletion” link in the Account & Data Deletion section. Some records may be retained where the law requires or permits it.

How do I contact support?

By email. See the Help & support section for the address and a button that opens your email app.
